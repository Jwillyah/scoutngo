# Coast cams on the Surf page

The Surf page lists the three public cams nearest the pin, within 30 mi, with
distance, direction and the way each looks. If there are none it says "No
public cams within 30 mi". Every cam is a link out: nothing is fetched,
framed or embedded from these pages (`tests/camsLinkOnly.test.ts`,
`tests/coastCams.test.ts`).

The data is `data/coast-cams.json`. `src/data/coastCams.ts` is generated from
it by `node scripts/build-coast-cams.mjs`.

## How it was gathered, and what that means

- **Surfchex.** From fetching surfchex.com and its llms.txt. Inland and
  soundside cams (the Intracoastal, downtown Wilmington, Southport) are left
  out: they do not show the surf.
- **Surfline.** surfline.com refuses automated requests (403), so every
  Surfline link is exactly as a search result gave it. Only spots whose
  result title said "Surf Cam" were kept. That a cam is live there rests on
  the title, not on loading the page. All are marked paid.
- **Everyone else.** From fetched index pages (SkylineWebcams state pages,
  HDOnTap, the NPS Olympic webcams page, the Delaware list) or search
  results. Each was checked with a request. Redirects were followed to where
  they land, and dead links (two EarthCam pages and five old Maine pages)
  were dropped.
- **Coordinates** are the pier's or beach's known position, approximate,
  not read from the cam page.
- **Facing** is the sea's direction from that beach. No page said which way
  its lens points.
- **Not covered.** Mississippi and Louisiana have no operator-run surf cams
  that could be found; Tybee Island likewise. WebCOOS, the federal coastal
  camera network, needs a login for its data.

## Refreshing it

1. Edit `data/coast-cams.json`: add, remove or correct entries. Keep the
   https URL exactly as the operator's page has it, and never guess a
   Surfline spot id.
2. Run `node scripts/build-coast-cams.mjs`. It rewrites
   `src/data/coastCams.ts` and prints the count per state; put the new counts
   in the table above.
3. `npx vitest run tests/coastCams.test.ts` checks every entry's fields,
   that the ids are unique, and that Wrightsville Beach still gets its own
   cams.

A cam page that has moved shows up as a dead link on the phone, not as an
error in the app: re-check the list every few months.

## VDOT traffic cams (Batch K, 3 Nov 2026)

`src/data/vdotCams.ts`, generated by `scripts/bake-vdot-cams.mjs` from the
list VDOT 511's public camera map loads
(`https://511.vdot.virginia.gov/services/511/map/array/cameras`; found by
watching that page's own requests, since its older paths and the Iteris data
API refuse without a token). Only cams in coastal Virginia (36.5-38.1 N,
75.2-77.1 W) are kept. VDOT labels each cam with the lanes it watches (NB,
WB...); that label is taken as where it looks, and cams labelled "Median" or
nothing are kept with facing null and never offered for "Look west". Links
open the cam's own still on vdotcameras.com on a tap; the app fetches
nothing. North Carolina's DriveNC API needs a developer key: no NC traffic
cams. Refresh: `node scripts/bake-vdot-cams.mjs`.

**Batch M, 8 Nov 2026: no longer offered for "Look west".** "Look WSW now"
offered I-264 cams labelled WB, 9 to 15 km inland, that looked the wrong
way: a lane label is the traffic a cam watches, not where its lens points.
Each VDOT cam now carries `facingIs: "lanes"`, which `src/core/skyCams.ts`
treats as an unknown facing, and an unknown facing is never offered. The one
rule for any row of cams toward the sun or the water lives there: a recorded
facing within 45° of the line (`CAM_FACING_DEG`) and within 15 km
(`CAM_MAX_KM`), else no row (`tests/camsTowardSun.test.ts`).

### Terms checked (Batch L, 3 Nov 2026)

Checked 2026-11-03 for whether linking to VDOT's camera pages is allowed.
Nothing found forbids a link; nothing grants one either. Kept: **link out
only**, the app fetches, frames and embeds no camera image

**The open question, for the owner.** VDOT's official channel for camera
data is SmarterRoads (smarterroads.org), behind a usage agreement and an
account. The 491 cams here were read from the public map's own request
instead, and each link goes straight to a cam's still on
snapshot.vdotcameras.com (now a 301 to `/<name>.png`), not to a 511 page.
Linking to a public image is not copying it, and the © notice is about
copying; but if VDOT's answer is that the list should come through
SmarterRoads, the bake script should switch to it or the cams come out.
Nobody at VDOT has been asked.
